## Research Overview

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Hydrology and Water Resources at Hohai University investigating **AI-driven downscaling of satellite-based water storage observations**, with applications to groundwater depletion monitoring and hydrological extremes in data-sparse regions. My research synthesizes **GRACE/GRACE-FO gravimetry**, **Google Earth Engine**, **remote sensing**, and **deep learning** to improve water availability assessments and support climate-resilient water resource management in South Asia and beyond.

**Core Research Interests:**
- Groundwater storage dynamics via satellite gravimetry
- Machine learning and deep learning for hydrological downscaling
- Remote sensing of surface water and wetland systems
- Hydroclimatic variability and extremes under climate change
- Water security and integrated water resource management
- Hydrological monitoring in data-sparse regions

## Research Highlights

### Groundwater Depletion Monitoring via Satellite Gravimetry
- Analyzed 20+ years of GRACE/GRACE-FO data to characterize groundwater storage changes
- Developed machine-learning downscaling algorithms to improve spatial resolution
- Identified hydrological hotspots of groundwater depletion and recovery patterns

### AI-Driven Hydrological Downscaling
- Implemented CNN and LSTM architectures for downscaling coarse-resolution satellite observations
- Integrated ancillary data (precipitation, land-use, soil properties) as model predictors
- Improved prediction accuracy by 35–40% compared to traditional statistical methods

### Wetland and Surface Water Dynamics
- Mapped flood-driven connectivity in data-sparse wetland systems using multi-temporal Landsat imagery
- Quantified hydrological variability driven by monsoon precipitation and land-use transitions
- Assessed impacts of large water bodies on downscaling methodologies

### Climate Change and Water Security
- Analyzed long-term precipitation and temperature trends under climate change scenarios
- Evaluated cascading risks to Pakistan's water security under compound extremes
- Contributed to climate adaptation and resilience planning frameworks
