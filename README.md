I am a hydrologist and water resources researcher focused on groundwater monitoring, satellite hydrology, remote sensing, and AI-driven hydrologic downscaling for sustainable water security. My work integrates geospatial analytics, machine learning, and satellite-based observations to improve understanding of water storage variability, hydrological extremes, and the impacts of climate and land-use change.

- Ph.D. candidate in Hydrology and Water Resources at Hohai University, Nanjing, China
- M.Sc. in Integrated Water Resource Management, MUET, Jamshoro
- B.Sc. (Hons.) in Agriculture Water Management, BZU, Multan
- Research focus: GRACE/GRACE-FO, Google Earth Engine, deep learning, remote sensing, groundwater, climate resilience
