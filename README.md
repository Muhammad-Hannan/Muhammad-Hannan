## 📋 Research Overview

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Hydrology and Water Resources at Hohai University with expertise in **satellite-based water resource monitoring**, **machine-learning downscaling algorithms**, and **remote sensing applications** for understanding hydrological systems in data-scarce regions. My research focuses on leveraging **GRACE/GRACE-FO gravimetry**, **Google Earth Engine**, and **deep learning** to quantify groundwater depletion, surface water dynamics, and climate-driven hydrological variability, particularly in South Asia.

**Research Interests:**
- Groundwater storage monitoring via GRACE satellite gravimetry
- AI/ML-based downscaling of hydrological variables
- Remote sensing for surface water and wetland monitoring
- Hydroclimatic variability under climate change
- Sustainable integrated water resource management (IWRM)
- Water security assessment in data-sparse regions
